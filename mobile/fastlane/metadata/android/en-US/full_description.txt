<h2>Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Unified Inbox, kanban and chat views</li>
  <li>IMAP/SMTP email and RSS/Atom feeds</li>
  <li>Threaded conversations with rich-text composing and media galleries</li>
  <li>Easy setup with OAuth or automatic mailbox discovery</li>
  <li>Encrypted local storage with credentials kept in the OS keyring</li>
  <li>Themes and 20+ languages</li>
</ul>

<h2>How it works</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Meron syncs your mail and feeds through a shared Rust core for speed and reliability.</li>
  <li>Your accounts and messages are stored encrypted on your device.</li>
  <li>When you enable sender images, Meron sends a SHA-256 hash of each sender's email address to Gravatar and, as a fallback, the sender's domain (and, if needed, its parent domain) to DuckDuckGo.</li>
</ul>
