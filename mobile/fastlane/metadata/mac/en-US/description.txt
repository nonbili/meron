Bring your email accounts together in Meron Mail. Read messages in a unified inbox, organize email on kanban boards, and follow conversations in a chat view. An RSS feed reader keeps blogs and news alongside your mail.

ONE INBOX FOR YOUR ACCOUNTS
Read mail from multiple accounts in one place or switch to an individual mailbox. Connect IMAP/SMTP accounts with OAuth sign-in where supported or automatic mailbox discovery.

ORGANIZE EMAIL ON A KANBAN BOARD
See mail in columns for folders, tags or saved searches. Use tags to group messages and rules to automate actions such as tagging, starring, archiving and moving mail.

READ AND REPLY LIKE A CONVERSATION
Choose chat or traditional conversation layouts. Keep replies together in threaded conversations, write with rich-text formatting, and browse attachments in media galleries.

FOLLOW RSS AND ATOM FEEDS
Read updates from your favorite blogs and publications in the same app as your email.

ENCRYPTED STORAGE AND OPTIONAL OPENPGP
Your local mail database is encrypted, with its key protected by Keychain. Enable OpenPGP per account to exchange encrypted email with recipients who have compatible keys. Ordinary email is not end-to-end encrypted by default.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose a theme and use Meron in more than 20 languages. Meron is open source and is available on Mac, iPhone and iPad.

ABOUT SENDER IMAGES
When you enable sender images, Meron sends a SHA-256 hash of each sender's email address to Gravatar and, as a fallback, the sender's domain (and, if needed, its parent domain) to DuckDuckGo.
